# Product Strategy Case Studies

A collection of product strategy case studies exploring customer problems, product opportunities, prioritization, experimentation, and business outcomes.

## Case Studies

### 01 — SaaS Activation

**Problem:**  
How might we improve activation for new SaaS customers?

**Focus areas:**

- Customer discovery
- Funnel analysis
- Jobs-to-be-done
- Opportunity identification
- Prioritization
- Product strategy
- Experimentation
- Success metrics

### 02 — Reducing SaaS Churn

Exploring how product teams can identify the drivers of customer churn and develop retention strategies.

### 03 — AI Product Opportunity

Exploring how AI can be applied to solve a meaningful customer problem while considering user value, feasibility, cost, and risk.

### 04 — Product-Led Growth

Exploring strategies for improving acquisition, activation, conversion, and expansion through product-led growth.
